# Research Narrative and Experimental Flow

## Research question

The project asks whether GPT-2 Small's apparent preference for correct answers to simple addition prompts is supported by a general, addition-specific internal mechanism. It also asks whether observed logit patterns can instead be explained by token baselines, prompt structure, repeated operands, or other learned associations.

The initial working account emphasized a heuristic-dominant, operator-sensitive pathway for apparent addition behavior. The analysis was designed to compare that account with behavioral baselines and competing explanations. The final account is narrower: the tested suite does not identify a general, reliably functioning addition mechanism; a target-dependent equal-operand preference is reported under selected conditions; its mechanism is unresolved. The operator-swap aggregates remain provisional because source prompt-level outputs are absent and the previous script used incomplete controls.

## Flow at a glance

Behavioral baseline → matched controls → token and unembedding baselines → corrected DLA → causal interventions → doubles effect → operator/connector substitutions → corpus proxy audit → next causal validation.

The first eight stages are completed or closed at the status indicated below. The final validation stage is a proposed next experiment; it was not completed in this project.

## Reading the narrative

The experiment did not move from a clean hypothesis to a single confirming test. An initial addition-circuit interpretation motivated component analysis. Numerical checks then questioned DLA scale and highlighted a static unembedding term. Better baselines and distribution-preserving interventions weakened specific head claims. The committed addition grid supports a parity pattern that changes with target range. A reported equal-operand preference remains, but the operator and corpus summaries cannot be independently reproduced from the current repository artifacts.

This sequence explains why the final claim is narrower than the starting hypothesis. The detailed correction history is in [audit_trail.md](audit_trail.md) (numbered research-relevant entries); the condensed chronology is in [RESEARCH_LOG.md](RESEARCH_LOG.md). This document presents the final reasoning path rather than retelling every debugging step.
