# Research Narrative and Experimental Flow

## Research question

The project began by asking whether GPT-2 Small's apparent preference for correct answers to simple addition prompts was supported by a general, addition-specific internal mechanism. It then tested whether the observed logit patterns could instead reflect token baselines, prompt structure, repeated operands, or other learned associations.

The initial working account proposed an addition-specific internal pathway. Behavioral baselines and controls narrowed that account: the tested suite does not establish a general addition mechanism. A target-dependent equal-operand preference remains measurable. Current clean-rerun exports add target-level inference, format/operator comparisons, intervention screens, and raw corpus records; they sharpen this phenomenon while leaving its mechanism unresolved. Operator exports are target-level summaries, not prompt-level scores.

## Flow at a glance

Initial circuit hypothesis → behavioral baseline → attribution and intervention audits → prompt/token structure → equal-operand scan → format/operator checks → corpus-proxy audit → exploratory component screen → evidence-bounded result and next causal test.

The stages below include the supplied clean-rerun outputs. The final stage remains proposed follow-up; the exported results do not establish a mechanism.

## Why the next question changed

The order of analyses follows the evidence rather than a preselected circuit narrative:

1. **A component story needs a behavior to explain.** M1 put a boundary around the starting claim: the all-pairs and held-out zero-shot cohorts scored 2/36 and 2/32, while the held-out constant guess scored 8/32. The study therefore did not treat a few positive target-vs-foil logits as evidence of general arithmetic competence.
2. **The first mechanistic signal needed a numerical audit.** Correct final LayerNorm scaling shrank previously inflated DLA attributions. Accounting for the unembedding bias reconciled selected contrasts and showed why the Run 2 `' 8'`/`' 1'` comparison on `158 + 274 =` was a token-score decomposition, not evidence that the model had computed answer 8.
3. **Candidate components needed interventions that preserve the operating regime.** Zero-ablation shifted residual scale and produced a larger L11H0 effect than mean-ablation. Separate patching checks lacked required anchors or hook validation. These findings narrowed specific component claims; they did not prove the absence of every arithmetic-related representation.
4. **The population map pointed to a more specific behavior.** The parity-sign pattern varied sharply by target range. Rather than force a single parity rule into a circuit explanation, the analysis compared equal operands with matched split controls and found a positive, target-dependent contrast in the saved digit+digit scores.
5. **Generalization and causal screens set the stopping point.** The seven-target sign-flip test is nominally significant (p = 0.03125; target-bootstrap 95% interval [+0.0738, +0.4374]). Digit+digit differs from digit+word after Holm correction (p = 0.046875), while the other format contrasts do not. Operator comparisons show no plus-versus-other difference after Holm correction (all p = 0.6875), which is not evidence of equivalence. Nominated components have one-sided FDR q = 0.09375 and two-sided q = 0.15; prospective-range effects are mixed. The endpoint is a bounded target-level pattern, not a confirmed circuit.

Taken together, the project contributes an evidence trail for a mechanistic case study: it records the initial hypothesis, shows why specific attribution and intervention claims failed their diagnostics, identifies the equal-operand contrast that remains, and defines tests that could distinguish repeated-operand computation from token and prompt-template effects.

## Reading the narrative

The project moved from an addition-circuit hypothesis to a bounded behavioral and mechanistic audit. Corrected DLA and distribution-preserving interventions narrowed specific component claims. The addition grid showed range-dependent parity alignment. A seven-target equal-operand contrast is positive on average (exact sign-flip p = 0.03125), with one format contrast surviving Holm correction. Operator tests detect no plus-versus-other difference, but are not evidence of equivalence. Candidate screens remain below the FDR threshold and prospective-range ablation effects are mixed. The raw corpus log is now available (79/0), but it is descriptive and does not test an association with model effects; historical correlations remain historical.

The progression is the result: an initial circuit account was tested, measurement and control problems were identified, stronger component interpretations were narrowed, and a target-dependent equal-operand pattern became the clearest follow-up target. The detailed correction history, including the final exploratory component screen, is in the single [audit trail](audit_trail.md). This document presents the research reasoning path and the evidence boundaries that shaped it.
