# Findings and evidence status

| Claim | Status | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| The tested suite demonstrates a general addition-specific mechanism in GPT-2 Small. | Not established | Behavioral cohorts; corrected attribution and intervention records; [`docs/RESEARCH_FLOW.md`](RESEARCH_FLOW.md) | The results do not show that no arithmetic-related representation exists. |
| Equal-operand prompts have a positive mean target-level advantage in the tested digit-addition scan. | Supported within the tested grid | [`equal_operand_primary_target_summary.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_primary_target_summary.csv); [`equal_operand_primary_inference.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_primary_inference.csv); audit A32 | Seven target levels; exact sign-flip p = 0.03125; target-bootstrap 95% interval [+0.0738, +0.4374]. This is not mechanistic evidence or broad generalization. |
| The effect is invariant to operand format. | Not established | [`equal_operand_format_advantages.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_format_advantages.csv); [`equal_operand_format_pairwise_tests.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_format_pairwise_tests.csv) | Digit+digit vs digit+word differs after Holm correction (p = 0.046875); remaining comparisons do not (Holm p = 0.21875). Prompt-level format scores are not included. |
| The effect is specific to addition, or operators are equivalent. | Not established | [`equal_operand_operator_advantages.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_operator_advantages.csv); [`equal_operand_operator_pairwise.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_operator_pairwise.csv); [`equal_operand_plus_vs_others.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_plus_vs_others.csv) | Plus-versus-other adjusted p = 0.6875; no detection is not proof of equivalence. Non-addition strings still score the arithmetic sum token. |
| The parity-matched control independently establishes a positive mean preference. | Not established | [`equal_operand_parity_control_inference.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_parity_control_inference.csv) | Mean +0.1865; exact sign-flip p = 0.140625; 95% target-bootstrap interval [−0.0068, +0.3863]. The interval includes zero. |
| The nominated components establish a causal circuit. | Not established | [`equal_operand_causal_specificity.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_causal_specificity.csv); [`equal_operand_new_range_target_summary.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_new_range_target_summary.csv) | Nominated components have one-sided FDR q = 0.09375 and two-sided q = 0.15; prospective range signed contrast decreases at 5/7 targets and increases at 2/7, with negative clean advantages at 26 and 34. |
| Corpus co-occurrence explains or rules out the equal-operand effect. | Unresolved | [`CORPUS_AUDIT.md`](CORPUS_AUDIT.md); raw logs and no-floor summary in `data/current/`; audit A32 | 79 requests, no logged failures, all seven joint log ratios positive; conditional ratios mixed. No association test or eligibility floor was applied; Dolma is a proxy. |
