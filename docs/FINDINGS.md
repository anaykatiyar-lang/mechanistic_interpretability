# Findings and claim status

This ledger separates measured results from interpretations and open questions. The notebook outputs are saved-run records; this repository has not rerun the final notebook from a clean environment.

| Claim | Status | Evidence | Scope and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| The tested benchmark does not show reliable general addition behavior. | Supported for the tested cohorts | [`paper/draft_paper.md`](../paper/draft_paper.md), Table 1; [`00_full_record.ipynb`](../notebooks/00_full_record.ipynb), M1 cells | All-pairs zero-shot: 2/36 (5.56%); held-out zero-shot: 2/32 (6.25%); held-out constant guess: 8/32 (25%). These are defined cohorts, not a universal capability claim. |
| The addition-grid logit-difference sign follows parity more often at lower sums. | Verified in the committed grid | [`addition_grid_79cell.csv`](../data/current/addition_grid_79cell.csv); [`addition_grid_heatmap.svg`](../figures/static/addition_grid_heatmap.svg) | 68/79 overall, 63/64 for sums ≤13, and 5/15 for sums ≥14. Rows share target sums and are not independent. |
| Equal-operand prompts have a target-dependent preference in the tested digit-addition scan. | Corroborated, exploratory | [`doubles_scan_scores.csv`](../data/current/doubles_scan_scores.csv); [`variance_scaling.csv`](../data/current/variance_scaling.csv); audit [A23](audit_trail.md#a23-doubles-analyses) | Mean advantage +0.247 (SE 0.102), positive at 6/7 targets. `adv/SD` is descriptive; there is one double prompt per target. |
| `10_mlp_out`, L9H9, and L10H2 explain the effect as a circuit. | Not established; candidates only | [`00_full_record.ipynb`](../notebooks/00_full_record.ipynb), cells 150–167; audit [A30](audit_trail.md#a30-final-notebook-mechanism-screen-and-corpus-log-status) | DLA FDR q=0.513; two-sided causal-screen FDR q=0.150. Transfer is mixed, the discovery targets were selected after the scan, and filler prompts also shift. |
| The candidate screen transfers beyond the discovery targets. | Exploratory, not independent confirmation | [`00_full_record.ipynb`](../notebooks/00_full_record.ipynb), cells 160–166 | Targets 8 and 14 were previously examined. The new 24–36 range is also exploratory: ablation reduces the advantage at five targets and increases it at two; no independent blind cohort was used. |
| Corpus frequency explains or rules out the equal-operand effect. | Unresolved | [`00_full_record.ipynb`](../notebooks/00_full_record.ipynb), raw collector cell; later uploaded notebook (8), raw collector cell; [corpus audit](CORPUS_AUDIT.md); audit [A26](audit_trail.md#a26-frequency-test-api-anchoring-statistic), [A30](audit_trail.md#a30-final-notebook-mechanism-screen-and-corpus-log-status), [A31](audit_trail.md#a31-later-corpus-collector-output) | Saved outputs report 79 attempts/0 failures and 80/1, respectively; both display the same seven-target summary. Per-attempt logs are unavailable, and neither raw collector reports a correlation. Earlier correlation summaries are historical. |
