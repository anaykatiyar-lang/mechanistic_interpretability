GPT-2 Small: research explorer

This study asks whether GPT-2 Small shows reliable addition behavior and whether a target-dependent equal-operand preference has an identified mechanism. The tested work does not establish a general addition circuit; the latest component screen is exploratory.

What the evidence supports

Behavior

On the tested cohorts, zero-shot top-1 accuracy was 2/36 and 2/32. The held-out constant guess scored 8/32.

Scoped to these prompts and cohorts.

Equal operands

The saved digit-addition scan reports a target-dependent preference, positive at 6/7 targets.

The mechanism remains unresolved.

Candidate components

10_mlp_out, L9H9, and L10H2 are exploratory candidates.

No confirmed circuit: DLA q=0.513; two-sided causal q=0.150.
