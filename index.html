From addition-circuit tracing to an equal-operand effect

An initial addition-circuit hypothesis led to checks of model behavior, attribution math, and causal interventions. Those checks narrowed the circuit claim and exposed a target-dependent equal-operand preference. The path below shows what evidence changed the question and what test could explain the measured pattern.

Research path: from circuit tracing to an equal-operand effect

Each stage shows the question, the diagnostic, what the result changed, and why the next analysis followed. The sequence makes the study’s change in direction inspectable.

  1. Starting question

    Does an apparent addition signal reflect a general internal circuit?

    Initial idea: A target-token preference on prompts such as 3 + 5 = appeared to offer a route to trace candidate heads and MLPs.

    Design consequence: The study first needed to establish the behavior and metric being explained. A component attribution is not, by itself, evidence that the model reliably performs the task.

  2. 1 · Establish the behavior

    Set a behavioral baseline before telling a circuit story.

    Measurement: Top-1 zero-shot accuracy was 2/36 on the all-pairs cohort and 2/32 on the held-out cohort. On that held-out set, a constant guess scored 8/32 (25%).

    Decision: These cohorts do not show reliable exact-answer performance above the simple held-out baseline. Keep cohort definitions distinct; do not generalize this benchmark result to every possible arithmetic representation.

  3. 2 · Reconcile the attribution

    Correct the scale and separate dynamic contribution from output bias.

    Diagnostic: Applying final LayerNorm scaling changed the DLA magnitudes; adding the unembedding-bias term reconciled selected logits. For 3 + 5 =, +0.4658 DLA plus +0.1782 bias totals +0.6440, within the reported +0.6443 residual. In Run 2, the ' 8' vs ' 1' contrast gives +0.8382 dynamic contribution and −1.0303 bias; the displayed terms sum to −0.1921, against the reported −0.1920.

    Decision: Run 2's foil is not the arithmetic answer to 158 + 274 (432). The contrast describes a token preference; it cannot show that an answer-8 computation was overridden.

  4. 3 · Challenge component causality

    Ask whether candidate-head effects survive controlled interventions.

    Diagnostic: Zero-ablation moved residual scale from σ=19.2002 to 23.1286 and produced an L11H0 shift of +0.0658. Mean-ablation across reference prompts kept σ near 19.0148 and reduced the shift to +0.0013. A two-component ablation was +0.8127 against an additive prediction of +0.8131 on that prompt.

    Decision: The earlier suppressor and interaction readings are not supported by these controls. Separate audits also found an unanchored operator-position metric and an unverified L9H9 path patch; those tests cannot establish the strong causal claims previously attached to them.

  5. 4 · Map task and prompt structure

    Check whether the apparent pattern is stable across the addition space.

    Measurement: In the committed 79-row grid, parity predicted the sign of the measured logit difference in 68/79 rows: 63/64 for sums ≤13, but 5/15 for sums ≥14.

    Decision: The pattern depends on target range, and grid rows share target sums. This is structured evidence about the prompts, not 79 independent demonstrations of an addition algorithm. It motivated testing a more specific repeated-operand contrast.

  6. 5 · Quantify the equal-operand pattern

    Compare each double with matched split-operand prompts for the same target.

    Measurement: The 39-row digit+digit scan evaluates both orders of split controls. For target T, each prompt score is L[T] − (L[T−1] + L[T+1])/2; the double’s score is then compared with the mean of matched split-control scores. The reported mean advantage is +0.247 (SE 0.102), positive at 6/7 target sums.

    Decision: This target-dependent preference is the clearest surviving result. There is one double prompt per target, and adv/SD is descriptive—not an inferential test.

  7. 6 · Probe the boundaries

    Test whether the result belongs specifically to addition or one formatting choice.

    Measurement: Format-level mean advantages for targets 8/10/12/16 are +0.418, +0.750, and +1.349 across digit+digit, digit+word, and word+word summaries. Operator/connector summaries report a largest raw advantage of +0.549 under “then” and a largest adv/SD of 6.15 under subtraction.

    Decision: The pattern is not specific to the addition string within these aggregate comparisons. Missing formatted/operator prompt-level scores limit recomputation; the normalized ratios do not establish statistical significance or universal operator blindness.

  8. 7 · Test the corpus-frequency explanation

    Check whether a public corpus proxy supports a verbatim-frequency account.

    Measurement: The final notebook collector uses the index field, avoids the extra anchor, handles failures explicitly, and records 79 query records with zero failed attempts. It reports no correlation; the raw CSV/JSONL logs are not included here. Earlier correlations are historical, and Dolma is only a proxy for GPT-2’s unavailable training corpus.

    Decision: The corpus check neither establishes nor rules out a frequency account. Raw logs and a prespecified eligibility rule/statistic are needed before drawing that conclusion.

  9. 8 · Return to mechanism with stricter tests

    Screen components against the measured equal-operand contrast.

    Measurement: The final notebook treats 10_mlp_out, L9H9, and L10H2 as exploratory candidates. DLA qFDR=0.513 and two-sided causal-screen qFDR=0.150; neither passes 0.05. Transfer is mixed: ablation reduces the advantage at previously examined target 8 and increases it at 14; in the new 24–36 range it decreases at five of seven targets and increases at two. Discovery targets were selected after the scan, and filler prompts also shift.

    Decision: These results do not validate a circuit. The printed removal fraction (+0.893908) also differs from the ratio of displayed aggregate means (about 0.664), so it is not used as a headline result without a defined aggregation.

  10. 9 · Contribution and next test

    The audit sharpened the research question.

    Study result: On these tests, a general addition-specific circuit is not demonstrated; a target-dependent equal-operand preference is measured in the saved prompts. The component screen did not identify its cause.

    Next study: Predefine independent targets and matched controls, retain prompt-level scores, use a held-out evaluation, and test necessity and restoration with distribution-preserving interventions. That design can distinguish a causal repeated-operand mechanism from token, template, and baseline effects.
