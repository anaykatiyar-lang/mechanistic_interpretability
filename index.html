From addition-circuit tracing to an equal-operand effect

An initial addition-circuit hypothesis led to checks of model behavior, attribution math, and causal interventions. Those checks narrowed the circuit claim and exposed a target-dependent equal-operand preference. The flow below shows how each result shaped the next question.

What the evidence supports

Behavior

On the tested cohorts, zero-shot top-1 accuracy was 2/36 and 2/32. The held-out constant guess scored 8/32.

Scoped to these prompts and cohorts.

Equal-operand pattern

The saved digit-addition scan reports a target-dependent preference, positive at 6/7 targets.

A measured behavioral pattern that motivates causal follow-up.

Candidate components

10_mlp_out, L9H9, and L10H2 are exploratory candidates.

No confirmed circuit: DLA q=0.513; two-sided causal q=0.150.
